About the Author

Ati Williams

Ati Williams is a nationally recognized general contractor, designer, and television personality who has made it her life's work to reimagine what home can mean at every stage of life. As the founder of Honeycomb Design Build, she has led hundreds of residential and commercial projects, blending art and science to deliver spaces that are as functional as they are beautiful.

Audiences first came to know Ati on Netflix's Hack My Home, where she showcased her creative approach to solving complex design challenges with heart and ingenuity. Off-screen, she is celebrated in the building industry as the author of the definitive guide to Universal Design for builders—a resource that has reshaped how professionals across the country think about accessible, future-ready spaces.

Ati's perspective is uniquely informed by her dual expertise in construction and design. As a licensed GC, she understands the technical precision required to execute complex projects. As a designer, she brings an intuitive sense of flow, aesthetics, and human experience. Her work has inspired homeowners, fellow contractors, and design professionals alike to see that aging in place is not about limitation, but about creating homes that are safer, smarter, and more beautiful at every age.

With Beautiful Transitions: A Design Guide for Aging in Place with Style and Grace, Ati invites readers into her philosophy of proactive, graceful design. She shares practical strategies, inspiring stories, and a roadmap for homeowners and professionals to create spaces that grow with us—spaces that celebrate independence, dignity, and joy.
