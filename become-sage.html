Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to become a certified Sage? +

Our program is designed for interior designers, architects, contractors, occupational therapists, and other professionals involved in home design and modification. You should have at least basic experience in your field and a passion for helping people age safely and beautifully at home.

How long does the certification process take? +

The comprehensive training program typically takes 6-8 weeks to complete, including online modules, live sessions, case study reviews, and the final certification exam. You can work at your own pace, but we recommend completing within 12 weeks for optimal learning retention.

What makes this certification different from CAPS? +

While CAPS focuses primarily on safety and accessibility, our certification emphasizes the integration of beautiful design with functional accessibility. We provide a complete business system, marketing support, and ongoing community that goes beyond traditional technical training.

Is there ongoing support after certification? +

Absolutely! Certified Sages receive ongoing support including monthly mastermind calls, access to our private community, continuing education opportunities, marketing resources, and priority placement in our client referral directory.

What's the return on investment? +

Most certified Sages recoup their investment within 3-6 months through higher project values, premium pricing, and increased client demand. Our average certified professional sees a 40% increase in project values and 25% increase in annual revenue within the first year.
